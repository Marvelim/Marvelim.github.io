# Why I keep notes here

This is where I collect notes, organized by course. Each note picks a course and
a title, and lands in that course's block on the [Notes](#/notes) page.

Notes are plain Markdown with LaTeX math, so I can drop an inline sum like $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ without any friction, or a display block when something deserves room to breathe:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}.$$
