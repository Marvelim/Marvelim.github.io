- The New Year Gala of the College of Chemistry and Molecular Engineering, Peking University.
- [Lu Chen](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lu_Chen_(magician)>)'s lecture at Tsinghua University.

You can watch some of my performances [at Tsinghua University](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BMHwz3Ee8/) and [at Peking University](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1vr421778j/?spm_id_from=333.1387.homepage.video_card.click).
